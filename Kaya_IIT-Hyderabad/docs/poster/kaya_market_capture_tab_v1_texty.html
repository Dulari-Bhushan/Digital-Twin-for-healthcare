4Why customers pay it, and how we compare
Why a ¥2,000 to ¥4,000 fee is easy to say yes to
Of every 100,000 patients, about 170 start dialysis each year. One dialysis patient costs the insurer about ¥5M a year.
If Kaya helps prevent 1 in 10 of them (17 people) over 5 years, the insurer saves ≈ ¥0.4B. Spread over 100,000 patients, a fee up to ≈ ¥4,000 pays for itself on dialysis alone.
What others charge
CureApp’s hypertension app: about ¥8,000 a month (insurance-reimbursed prescription app). BeatO’s coaching program in India: ₹1,599 a month. Kaya’s ¥2,000 a year is about ¥170 a month, paid by the insurer or hospital chain, not by the patient.
Assumed: 1 in 10 prevented, 5 years of dialysis cost, ¥150 = US$1, ₹84 = US$1. Not yet tested in Japan.
